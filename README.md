# 📈 Multi-Asset Portfolio Tracker

A robust and flexible portfolio tracking tool designed to monitor and analyze investments across multiple asset classes and international markets. Keep all your holdings—from global equities to digital assets and local funds—organized in one place.

## 🌟 Key Features

* **🇺🇸 US Stock Market:** Track equities, ETFs, and market performance denominated in USD.
* **🇹🇼 Taiwan Stock Market (TWSE/TPEx):** Monitor Taiwanese stocks and local market holdings.
* **🪙 Cryptocurrency:** Real-time tracking of crypto assets, coins, and tokens across wallets/exchanges.
* **🇲🇾 Malaysia Fund Market:** Keep an eye on local Malaysian unit trusts, funds, and Bursa Malaysia investments.
* **📊 Performance Analytics:** Gain insights into portfolio allocation, asset distribution, and overall returns.
