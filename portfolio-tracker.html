Prices refresh every 0.5 seconds: US and Taiwan quotes from Yahoo Finance (may be delayed), crypto from Binance (USDT pairs), mutual funds are manual. Cost is converted at the current FX rate, so currency gains and losses are not tracked separately. The 5-year high is built from monthly highs (Yahoo Finance, Binance) and rechecked every 6 hours. For a mutual fund, the highest NAV you have entered is used as its ATH. Rows shown first are sample data: edit or delete them.
