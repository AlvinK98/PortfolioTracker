Add holding or record a trade

Buying a ticker you already hold blends the units and WAC. Selling adds to realized P/L (in MYR at today’s rate) and removes the row when nothing is left. A split multiplies your units and divides WAC and ATH by the ratio, then pulls the new live price (or use the New price field).

Group by
  1. Prices refresh at the Stock refresh interval below: US, UK and Asia-Pacific stock quotes from Yahoo Finance, crypto from Binance (USDT pairs), mutual funds are manual.
  2. Cost is converted at the current FX rate, so currency gains and losses are not tracked separately.
  3. The 5-year high and its date are built from daily highs and rechecked every 6 hours.
  4. For a MY mutual fund, the highest NAV you have entered is used as its ATH.
  5. YTD % compares the current price with the last close of the previous year. Manual-price funds show a dash.
  6. MoM, QoQ and YoY % compare the current price with the last close on or before the same date 1 month, 3 months and 12 months ago. Funds and tickers without enough history show a dash.
  7. UK quotes in pence (GBp) are converted to pounds (GBP). A London-listed fund that trades in another currency, such as USD, is still valued as GBP.
  8. When grouped by Market, the trading hours beside each market name are in Malaysia time (MYT). They turn yellow while that market is open, and prices in open markets are also shown in yellow. Lunch breaks (Hong Kong, Japan, China) are not shown.

Your data stays on your device

This tracker has no accounts and no server. Your holdings, transactions, targets and settings are saved only in your own browser (local storage). All data is stored on your local device and no data is sent to the developer.

What we collect

Nothing. This tracker uses no analytics tracking or advertising trackers, and it does not ask for personal information.

Tracking only

This tracker is for tracking only. It does not provide trading or investment advice, and it does not have access to any brokerage account.

Market data

All data comes from Binance and Yahoo Finance. The data is not 100% accurate and may be delayed.

Third-party services

To show prices, this page requests ticker symbols from Binance and Yahoo Finance (through relay services) and exchange rates from open.er-api.com. Those providers may receive your IP address, browser details and the ticker symbols requested, under their own privacy policies. Your units, costs and balances are never sent.

Your control

You can edit or delete any holding or transaction at any time. Clearing your browser’s site data permanently removes everything saved here. Data is not backed up, so use Export to keep your own copy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
